The name

From family joke to pizza setup.

The name came from home. The grown-ups would finish the kids’ leftover pizza crusts and became known as the Crust Monsters.

It stuck. Now it is the name for a small mobile pizza setup with the same simple idea behind it: make pizzas where the crust is not the bit people leave behind.

I’m genuinely passionate about great pizza — from choosing the best ingredients and understanding the flour, to dialling in the dough, bake and flavour combinations. The creative side matters to me, but the best bit is seeing people enjoy something I’ve made.

The pizza

Traditional methods. Fast service. Serious crust.

The dough starts with a biga preferment, a traditional Neapolitan-style method that builds deeper flavour, better texture and a crust with real chew.

It is made with Tipo 00 flour, San Marzano DOP tomatoes and fior di latte mozzarella, then cooked hot in around 90 seconds. That means pizzas come out quickly, fresh and ready for hungry guests.

Pizza dictionary

Neapolitan-style, in plain English.

Neapolitan pizza has a few words that sound more complicated than they are. Here is the plain-English version.

Neapolitan-style

A soft, light pizza style with a thin centre, simple toppings and a puffy crust.

Biga preferment

A pre-fermented dough mix that develops richer flavour, better texture and a more interesting crust.

DOP

A protected origin mark used on certain Italian ingredients, including proper San Marzano tomatoes.

San Marzano DOP tomatoes

Sweet, rich tomatoes used for a simple sauce that does not need hiding under too many toppings.

Fior di latte

A creamy cow’s milk mozzarella that melts softly across the pizza and is traditionally used in pizzerias across Naples.

Tipo 00 flour

Finely milled flour used for a lighter dough, good crust structure and a satisfying chew.

Cornicione

The raised outer crust. The bit the Crust Monster was basically built around.

Leopard spotting

The small dark spots on the crust from a hot bake. They add texture, flavour and character.

Fast bake

A very hot, quick cook — around 90 seconds — so pizzas come out fresh without keeping guests waiting.

Hand-stretched

The dough is shaped by hand rather than rolled flat, helping keep the crust airy.

Booking terms

Clear terms, no surprises.

The important bits in plain English: how bookings are confirmed, what happens if plans change, and how we handle weather.

Booking and payment

A 50% deposit is due 6 weeks before the event to confirm the booking. The remaining balance is due 1 week before the event.

For bookings made within 6 weeks of the event, the 50% deposit is due when the booking is confirmed. For bookings made within 1 week of the event, full payment is due when the booking is confirmed.

Cancellation policy

More than 6 weeks before the event: no cancellation charge.

6 weeks to 2 weeks before the event: the 50% deposit is retained.

Less than 2 weeks before the event: 75% of the total booking value is retained/payable.

Less than 7 days before the event: the full booking balance is retained/payable.

Where possible, we’ll try to move your booking to another suitable date instead of treating it as a cancellation, subject to availability.

Weather

Wet weather is not normally a problem. We can bring cover and operate in most conditions, provided there is a safe outdoor space to set up and cook.

We may need to pause, move or cancel service if conditions become unsafe, such as extreme wind, severe weather warnings, flooding, unsafe ground conditions, or anything that makes cooking or serving unsafe.

Dietary requirements

Vegan options are possible. Gluten-free bases are not currently offered.
